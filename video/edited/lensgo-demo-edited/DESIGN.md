# LensGo Demo Visual System

## Visual direction

The video combines LensGo's warm orange mobile identity with the jade-and-paper visual language of the hotel service. The result should feel calm, capable, privacy-first, and suitable for a competition demo rather than a consumer advertisement.

## Palette

- Ink: `#17231F`
- Paper: `#F5F4EF`
- Card: `#FFFEFA`
- Jade: `#1F6F59`
- Jade dark: `#164D3F`
- Mint: `#DCECE5`
- LensGo orange: `#FF7F16`
- Coral: `#DD6F54`
- Interface gray: `#656D78`
- Hairline: `#DFE3DC`

## Typography

Use `Microsoft YaHei UI`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif for all Chinese text. Titles are medium weight and spacious; interface captions are compact and precise. Use numeric labels as oversized editorial anchors.

## Layout

- 1920×1080, 16:9.
- Phone footage sits inside a restrained device frame, never cropped.
- Website screenshots use large cards with 28px radii and soft shadows.
- Preserve generous negative space and avoid dense dashboard montages.
- Use one primary focal point per scene.

## Motion

- Calm 450–700ms fades and lateral reveals.
- Use jade-to-paper wipes between product areas.
- Website screenshots receive subtle 1.03× push-ins.
- Orange focus rings and short callout leaders highlight controls.
- Captions use hard in/out cuts for readability.

## Captions

Captions sit in a dark translucent rail at the bottom, centered, with no more than two Chinese lines. Key nouns may be orange; all other caption text is white.

## Source fidelity

The hotel visuals follow the captured `#17231F`, `#F5F4EF`, `#1F6F59` system. The data publisher follows its captured white/gray panels with `#FF7F16` accent and the five-step crowd color legend. Captured UI screens must remain unaltered except for framing, scaling, and focus overlays.
